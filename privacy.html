Privacy Notice

Effective November 8, 2026. Rizzalian is an independent project, not affiliated with any school or university.

1. Who we are

Rizzalian is a dating web app for adults (18+). For questions or requests about your data, contact us at YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL.

2. What we collect

  • Account: email address, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), and your birthday.
  • Profile (what you choose to add): username, name, gender, location, height, hobbies, bio, "looking for", course, graduation year, profession, profile prompts and photo.
  • Activity: likes, passes, matches, messages, blocks and reports you make.
  • Technical: basic logs our providers keep for security and operation.

3. Why we use it

  • To create your account and verify you are 18 or older.
  • To show your profile to other adult users, make matches and deliver messages.
  • To keep the community safe (handling reports and blocks, removing abusive accounts).
  • To produce anonymous, aggregate statistics about how the app is used.

By creating an account you consent to this processing. We do not sell your data.

4. Who can see what

  • Other users can see your profile details listed in section 2 (except email, password and birthday). They see your age, not your birthday.
  • Only you and your match can read your messages.
  • Moderators can read reports and may review related content to act on a report.
  • Do not put information in your profile that you do not want other users to see.

5. Service providers

We use Supabase to host our database, authentication and photo storage, and an email provider to send account emails. They process data on our behalf. Your data may be stored on servers outside the Philippines.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account (My Profile, then Delete Profile) your profile, likes, matches and messages are deleted. Reports about abuse you made or received may be removed with the account, and provider backups may keep copies for a limited time before they expire.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) you have the right to be informed, to access and correct your data, to object, to erasure or blocking, to data portability, and to claim damages. You can edit your profile in the app, delete your account in the app, or email us for any request. You may also complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

8. Security

We use access controls so users can only read what they are allowed to see, and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Age

Rizzalian is only for people aged 18 and older. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this notice and will change the date above when we do.
